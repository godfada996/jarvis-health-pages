JARVIS Health — Privacy Policy

Effective November 15, 2026. Personal-use integration; implementation in development.

JARVIS Health is a private, single-user project for viewing and analyzing the owner's Oura Ring data. Not affiliated with Oura.

Data requested and purpose

With explicit Oura authorization, the planned integration may access approved sleep, readiness, activity, heart-rate, blood-oxygen, stress, and other selected wellness data. It is intended to create personal reports, charts, and trends, not advertising or data sales.

Storage and sharing

The planned design stores health records and OAuth credentials locally, with access controls. The integration has not yet been implemented or security-tested. If external AI analysis is added, the provider and data transmitted will be disclosed and the owner must opt in before health data is sent. Oura independently processes information under its own policies.

Control and deletion

The owner will be able to disconnect Oura and separately delete locally imported health data. Disconnecting does not automatically delete prior imports.

Security and updates

Health records, tokens and secrets are not intended to be published in GitHub or these public pages. No security guarantee is offered while the project is under development. This policy will be updated before use by others.
